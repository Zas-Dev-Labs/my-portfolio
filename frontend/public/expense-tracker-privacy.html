Privacy Policy
Expense Tracker

This Privacy Policy describes how ZasDevLabs (operated by Sashi Kiran Rao) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Expense Tracker Android application.

Effective Date: June 2025 · Last Updated: June 2025 · App Version: 1.0

1 Overview

Expense Tracker is a personal finance application that helps you manage and track your expenses across multiple bank accounts and payment apps. The app is designed with your privacy in mind — it can operate fully offline, and we collect only what is strictly necessary to provide core functionality.

2 Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account or log in, we collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials. This information is used solely to identify your session and secure your data.
  • Expense & Financial Data: Transaction records, amounts, categories, dates, and notes that you manually enter or capture within the app. This data is stored on your device and, where applicable, synced to our secure servers.
  • Camera Access: The app requests access to your device camera to capture photos of receipts or expense documents. Images are processed locally on your device and used only for the purpose you initiate. We do not store or transmit raw camera images to our servers without your explicit action.
  • Device Information: Basic device identifiers (OS version, device model) may be collected for analytics and crash reporting purposes.

3 How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Authenticate and maintain your account session
  • Store, organize, and display your expense records
  • Enable receipt capture via the device camera
  • Provide offline access to your financial data
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs through anonymized analytics
  • Respond to support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4 Offline Functionality & Data Storage

Expense Tracker is built to work fully offline. Your expense data is stored locally on your device first, ensuring access at all times without an internet connection.

When connectivity is available, data may be synced to secure cloud storage to enable backup and multi-device access. All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Locally stored data is protected by your device's built-in security mechanisms.

5 Analytics

The app uses limited analytics to understand general usage patterns, identify crashes, and improve stability. At the current stage, analytics collection is minimal and does not include personally identifiable information.

Analytics data is anonymized and aggregated. It is not used to identify individual users. You may opt out of analytics in the app's settings.

6 Camera Permission

The app requests CAMERA permission to allow you to photograph receipts and expense documents directly within the app. This permission is used only when you actively initiate a capture action.

The app does not access the camera in the background, and captured images are not shared with any third party without your explicit action.

7 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers who assist in operating the app (e.g. cloud infrastructure, analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • To protect the safety or rights of users or the public

8 Data Retention

Your expense data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. Locally stored data on your device must be removed manually.

9 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw camera or storage permissions at any time via device settings
  • Opt out of analytics within the app settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at skr@zasdevlabs.tech.

10 Children's Privacy

Expense Tracker is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used this app and provided personal data, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove that information.

11 Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit, secure authentication protocols, and access controls on our servers. However, no system is 100% secure — we encourage you to use a strong password and keep your device software up to date.

12 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. When we do, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. For significant changes, we will notify you via an in-app message or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
