1 Overview & Application Scope

The Kanthastha ("Kantastha: Recite & Memorize") Android mobile application ("the App") is developed and operated by ZasDevLabs (led by Sashi Kiran Rao). The App is crafted to assist spiritual practitioners, students, and language enthusiasts in reciting and mastering classical Sanskrit Slokas, Stotras, and Mantras.

We believe deeply in user privacy. The App is built on a local-first architecture, meaning your custom chants, stanza notes, and voice recordings remain on your physical Android device.

2 Information We Collect & Process

We process only the minimal data strictly required to deliver the core features:

  • Custom Sloka Library: Slokas, stanzas, translations, transliterations, and categories that you add through the interface are saved directly in your device's internal SQLite / Room database.
  • Voice Recordings: User audio clips recorded during pronunciation practice are stored locally in the app's private sandbox storage directory.
  • Text-to-Speech Requests: Chunked phrases sent to your device's native Android Text-To-Speech engine to synthesize guide audio.
  • Non-Personal Technical Logs: Standard anonymous crash logs provided via Google Play Developer Console (e.g., OS version, device manufacturer, stack traces).

We do not collect: Your name, email, physical location, contacts, device identifiers for marketing, or financial information.

3 Android Permissions & Usage

The App requests only necessary runtime permissions:

  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone): Required exclusively for the pronunciation practice module, enabling you to record your recitation of sloka chunks and listen back to verify your cadence and pronunciation.
  • Internal App Storage: Used to persist your custom sloka collections, learning bookmarks, and audio clips securely in the private app sandbox.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used only for checking optional standard library updates or loading external support links.

You may grant or revoke the microphone permission at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Sloka Companion > Permissions.

4 Voice & Microphone Data Protection

Because our app includes audio recording features, we enforce strict voice privacy protections:

  • Zero Cloud Voice Uploads: Audio recordings made during pronunciation practice are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to remote servers or cloud storage.
  • No Voiceprint or Biometric Profiling: We do not extract biometric voiceprints, identify speakers, or build voice recognition profiles.
  • No AI Model Training: Your voice is never used to train third-party AI or speech synthesis models.
  • Immediate Deletion: You can discard or overwrite any recording at will within the app interface.

5 Data Storage, Retention & Security

All user-generated content and recordings reside in Android's protected application sandbox. Other applications installed on your device cannot access this data without explicit authorization.

Complete Data Deletion: If you clear the app storage or uninstall the App from your device, all custom slokas, practice logs, and audio recordings are permanently deleted.

6 Third-Party Services

The App interacts with the following system components:

  • Android Native Text-to-Speech: The speech engine built into your Android operating system (e.g. Google Speech Services, Samsung TTS).
  • Google Play Services: For secure app distribution, license verification, and updates.

The App contains no third-party advertising SDKs or user tracking frameworks.

7 Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

The Sloka Learning App is safe and educational for users of all ages, including young students learning cultural hymns. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

8 User Control & Data Rights

You have complete control over all data created in the app. You may edit, categorize, or delete any sloka, stanza chunk, or audio recording directly through the user interface at any time.
